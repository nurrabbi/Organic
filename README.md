“Smart groceries management made simple — from stock to sales.
All‑in‑one grocery business control system.
Your complete grocery enterprise solution.”

The Organic - Groceries Management System is a comprehensive enterprise web application built with Laravel to streamline grocery business operations. It integrates inventory control, supplier management, employee payroll, customer orders, and financial reporting into one unified platform.

Key Highlights:

Manage products, warehouses, and stock transactions.

Handle customer carts, orders, shipments, invoices, and payments.

Track supplier relationships and purchase orders.

Automate sales summaries, profit/loss reports, and customer segmentation.

Secure user and employee management with detailed logs.# Organic

# **Used in Project**

* **Laravel 13 | PHP 8.4 | Node.js 26.8.1 | Composer 2.9.5 | MySQL | Mailtrap**
* **Templetes: Organic(frontend), Sneat(Backend) {Open source}**
