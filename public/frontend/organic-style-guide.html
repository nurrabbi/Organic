04 · Future design rule

Use the Organic palette, not just the colors

Primary

#6BB252 should be the most visible action/accent color.

Contrast

#364127 gives headings, labels and navigation a natural deep-green anchor.

Softness

Use cream, soft green and soft peach surfaces instead of saturated blocks everywhere.
